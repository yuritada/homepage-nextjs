The slides from the first time I ever spoke at an event outside my university. *Written in Japanese.*

I was a first-year student then, and about all I could do was the Python we had touched in class. The talk looks back at why "Reverse Akinator," the project from my first hackathon, took a company award — the UX of letting the AI answer only yes or no — and lays out the technologies I saw other participants using, sorted into languages, frontend, tooling, and concepts, as a list of things I wanted to learn next.

The slides are rough by any standard, but I keep them up as the starting point everything since has been built on.
