# I Started a Blog

I added a blog to my portfolio site.

## Why I Started a Blog

As a record of my job hunt and my everyday learning, here's what I want to write about:

- Notes on technical things I've been thinking through
- What I'm learning and what I'm working on
- Thoughts on study groups and events I attend
- Random things I happen to notice

I've found that turning input into output is what actually organizes my thinking. So I'm going with an "understand it by writing it" approach.

## The Tech Stack

This blog runs on Next.js + Markdown. Posts are managed as `.md` files, with `gray-matter` parsing the frontmatter and `react-markdown` handling the rendering.

```markdown
---
title: "Post title"
date: "2026-05-01"
tags: ["Tag 1", "Tag 2"]
summary: "One-line summary"
---

Write the body here
```

It's a simple setup, but it's version-controlled and genuinely pleasant to work with.

## What's Next

I'm planning to write broadly — technical posts, write-ups from events I attend, everyday realizations. Thanks for reading!
